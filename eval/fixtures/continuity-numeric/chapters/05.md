Mara Voss carried a five-year-old girl across the yard without slowing. Harrow Keep rose above the courtyard in dark, uneven tiers. Harrow Keep was a seven-story fortress with a single gate. In Harrow Keep, the fourth story held the armory, and the guards there did not look up. Mara Voss crossed the yard and reached the gate before the bell rang. The gatekeeper leaned out of his box and counted her steps with his eyes. She gave him no answer, and he asked for none. Behind her the wind pulled at the banners, and somewhere inside a door slammed shut.
