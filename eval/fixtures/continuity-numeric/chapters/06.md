Ironwatch kept only nine guards on the wall that night. The rest of the garrison slept in the lower barracks, and nobody woke them. Harrow Keep was built in 1206, according to the old ledgers. The ledgers were water-stained and half of them were missing. Harrow Keep fell in 1290, and the ledgers say nothing of why. Mara Voss turned the last page and closed the book. The candle beside her had burned down to a stub. Outside, the watch changed with a low call and a scrape of boots. She blew out the flame and listened to the dark settle over the keep.
