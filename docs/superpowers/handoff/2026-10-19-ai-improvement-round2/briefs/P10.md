## Goal

1. Two pure predicates in a new module `src/ai/evidence-anchor.ts`: "does this evidence sentence name the fact's subject" and "does this evidence sentence share a keyword with the thread".
2. `extractionToProposals` (src/ai/extraction.ts) takes an optional mode `evidenceAnchor?: 'off' | 'count' | 'drop'` in `ExtractionContext` (default `'off'` = today's behaviour, byte for byte). It returns a new top-level field `weak: { weakFactEvidence: number; weakThreadEvidence: number }`. In `'count'` it only counts. In `'drop'` it also leaves the item out.
3. `runChapterCheck` (src/server/chapter-check.ts) runs the mapper in `'count'` and adds the two counts to `ChapterCheckResult.extraction`. The eval `check` step already writes that object to `chapters/NN.extraction.json`; `eval/novel/steps/finish.ts` reads it and sums it into `run-summary.json` (`extraction.totals`, `extraction.byChapter`), with the two keys optional so old files still parse.

No prompt text changes. No proposal changes. The author sees exactly what they saw before.

Counting every review flag (duplicates and bible conflicts too) the rates are 11 of 47 (23%) weak against 5 of 20 (25%) anchored: no separation.

Conclusion: the checks are cheap and have high recall on evidence defects, but dropping on them costs far more good facts than bad ones removed. Count now, decide with R2b numbers (and a per-item look at the weak list) before any drop.

## Design (do in this order, TDD per step: write the test, watch it fail, then implement)

1. **Pure module** (`src/ai/evidence-anchor.r2.test.ts` first, then `src/ai/evidence-anchor.ts`). Exports below; helpers are private.

   Shared helpers:
   - `normalizeForMatch(s)`: curly single quotes (U+2018, U+2019) to `'`, curly double quotes (U+201C, U+201D) to `"`, runs of whitespace to one space, trim, `toLowerCase()`.
   - `containsTerm(text, term)` (both already normalized): false for an empty term. A term with a Hangul syllable matches as a plain substring (a particle follows the name directly). Any other term matches at an index whose previous character is not a letter or digit (`/[\p{L}\p{N}]/u`), so "key" does not match inside "monkey"; there is no right-side check, so "key" matches "keys".
   - `HAS_HANGUL = /[가-힣]/`.

   `subjectForms(names, kind)`: the normalized forms a sentence may use for a card.
   - `kind` is the card's `EntryKind` or undefined. `personLike = kind === undefined || kind === 'character'`.
   - For each name in `names` (trimmed): skip when it has fewer than 2 characters (`Array.from(name).length`). Add the whole name.
   - A name with 2 or more whitespace-separated words also adds each word. Strip a trailing `'s` or `'` from the word first (after turning U+2019 into `'`). A word with Hangul is added when it has 2 or more characters. A Latin word is added when it is only `a-z` after lowercasing, has at least 4 letters for a person-like card and at least 3 for any other kind, and is not `the`, `and` or `for`. (The 4-letter rule keeps a shared family name like "Han" from tying "Han Seoyeon" to "Han Serin".)
   - A one-word, all-Hangul name of a person-like card adds its tail: length 3 adds `name.slice(1)` (한세린 -> 세린); length 4 adds `name.slice(1)` and `name.slice(2)` (남궁민수 -> 궁민수, 민수).
   - Forms are de-duplicated, in first-seen order.

   `evidenceNamesSubject(evidence, names, kind)`: `true` when `subjectForms` is empty (nothing to check against); else `true` when some form `containsTerm` the normalized evidence.

   `threadKeywords(text)`: tokens of `text` (replace U+2019 with `'`, split on `/[\s,.;:!?"“”‘’()[\]{}/·–—~…-]+/u`, strip leading and trailing `'`). A token with Hangul: `stripJosa(token)` (from `@/lib/korean/josa`, up to two particles, keeps at least 2 characters), lowercase, kept when it has 2 or more characters and is not in `KEYWORD_STOP_KO`. Any other token: lowercase, strip a trailing `'s`, kept when it is only `a-z`, has 3 or more letters and is not in `KEYWORD_STOP_EN`. De-duplicated, in order.

   ```ts
   const KEYWORD_STOP_KO = new Set(['아직', '않았다', '않은', '않는', '있는', '없는', '드러나지', '드러난', '그의', '그녀', '대해', '때문', '이후', '모두', '다시', '그리고', '하지만', '그래서'])
   const KEYWORD_STOP_EN = new Set(('the and for with from into onto upon are was were been being its this that these those who whom whose what which when where why how whether then than but not nor yet still also just only even very too any all some each both either neither one two has have had having did does done doing can could will would shall should may might must get got let her his she him they them their there here about after before between over under down out off again once while because since until though although else ever never every other another such own same more most much many few less').split(' '))
   ```

   `evidenceHasThreadKeyword(evidence, texts)`: keywords = `texts.flatMap(threadKeywords)`; `true` when there are none; else `true` when some keyword `containsTerm` the normalized evidence.

## Gating

- Not a local-only change and not an option: it runs for every provider, because the check is a pure function of the merged `Extraction`. It cannot change a proposal: the product mode is `'count'`, and `'off'` is the default for every other caller, so the interview and import paths and every existing test see the old behaviour byte for byte.
- Cloud default path: no prompt change, no proposal change, no extra AI call, no extra database access. The only visible differences are two extra integers in `ChapterCheckResult.extraction` (not shown to users) and in the eval summary.
- The `'drop'` mode exists only for tests and for a later decision. To turn it on, change the one constant `EVIDENCE_ANCHOR` in `src/server/chapter-check.ts`; nothing else.

## Risks

- A 70% weak share for facts reads like a bug in the extractor. It is not: it is how Korean fiction is written. Do not tune the extraction prompt toward the check without evidence (see Open questions).
- The counts are approximate by design. Facts are counted after the duplicate checks. A thread is counted before later steps may drop it (already on the wiki, already pending), so `weakThreadEvidence` can include a thread that never became a proposal.
- The R2a runs (made before this code) have no counters. For R2a, and for any earlier run, recount offline with `weak_evidence.py` (same algorithm, reads the saved `chapter-extract` calls; it lives in the scratchpad next to this brief, outside the repo, so the orchestrator copies it for the results writer). It rebuilds card aliases from the saved prompt, so live R2b counts and offline recounts can differ by a few items; compare them as rough levels, not exact numbers.
- Flag counts in Evidence are my reading of six reviews and a sample of 67 facts; the precision figures are indicative only.
- English names: a family name shared by two characters ("Han") is deliberately not a form, so a sentence that names only "Han" does not anchor either card. A real alias fixes that case.
- `kind` is undefined for a pending card whose entry the extraction does not repeat; such a card is treated as person-like (4-letter Latin words, Hangul tail forms). This only lowers the weak count a little.
- The HX5 assertion change is the only edit to an existing test. If another track edits the same test in the same worktree, merge by keeping both key sets.
- `chapter-check.ts` is edited by P3 and P14 too. The P10 hunks are: the imports, the `ExtractionCounts` line, the new constant, the `extractionToProposals` context object, and the `extraction:` object of the return. Do not reformat the rest of the file.

## Open questions (defaults chosen)

- Drop or count? Default: count (the spec says drop; the saved runs say it would remove about 36 good facts to remove 9 defective ones). Suggested rule for a later round, not for this one: turn on `'drop'` for facts only if, in at least two cloud rounds and at least 40 weak facts, the share of weak facts that the reviews call evidence-defective is at least three times the share among anchored facts, and the drop would remove less than 30% of the facts.
- Interview and import extraction? Default: not checked (`'off'`). The interview answer rarely repeats the subject (48% weak on the saved runs), and the import path surfaces no counters.
- Thread keywords from the title only, or title plus description? Default: both (title only flags 8 of 19, mostly fine threads).
- Check the title and description of the existing thread when the model gives `existingThreadRef`? Default: no; the model copies the title, and the extra lookup would make the offline recount differ from the live count.
- A prompt rule that asks the model to quote a sentence that names the subject ("evidence에는 대상 이름이 든 문장을 고른다")? Default: no. It changes `CHAPTER_EXTRACT_SYSTEM`, the three parts prompts, the English systems snapshot and the speech-off golden strings, and it is a guess that it would improve facts. Decide after the R2b weak counts.
- Show the weak flag to the author in the proposal review? Default: no (new payload field and UI, and 70% of facts would carry it).
- A per-item list of weak items in `chapters/NN.extraction.json` so a reviewer can judge precision? Default: no; it would need a reviewer-instruction change owned by M3.
