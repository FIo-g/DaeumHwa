## 3. How the eval harness can measure each metric

- Name truncation: a proposal name that is a proper substring of an existing card or pending card name, from proposals in the DB or from the decisions log. Computable offline. MISSING: no field in run-summary.
- Evidence verbatim: for each extracted fact/thread/relation, locateQuote(source, evidence) on the source the call read. Source for interview is the `<answer>` block in CallRecord.prompt (eval-client.ts:40-70). Source for chapter is `<manuscript>`. - Undecided-only: regex on the answer (answer text trimmed equals "아직 정하지 않았어요." or a few variants) plus count of facts in that call's output. Computable offline.
- Planted-as-developed: for each chapter-extract call with a planned thread "(계획됨)" in <open_threads>, check whether output threads with that existingThreadRef have kind "developed" when they are first planted. Computable offline from prompt and output.
- Overall: a new section in metrics.ts (extractionMetrics) fed by calls and decisions, and a field in buildRunSummary.
