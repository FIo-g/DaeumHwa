# How major literary prizes define excellent fiction

**Short answer:** Only the Nobel, Booker, International Booker, Women's Prize, and Pulitzer publish anything close to a quality standard. The Nobel standard is the will's vague "ideal direction" wording, with no written rubric. The Pulitzer is defined by nationality and subject. The Women's Prize has three named tenets. The Hugo, Nebula, and Edgar rules define eligibility (length or format), not quality. The National Book Award leaves criteria entirely to each panel.

## 1. Nobel Prize in Literature

**1a. Alfred Nobel's will (1895)**
- Swedish original: <citation refs="aFlVO2HFO4NCwU0yBJOGp">"en del den som inom litteraturen har producerat det utmärktaste i idealisk rigtning"</citation>
- English translation (Jeffrey Ganellen, 2018): <citation refs="AhB6P16duH9vOtTcDrq1G">"one part to the person who, in the field of literature, produced the most outstanding work in an ideal direction"</citation>
- Sources: https://www.nobelprize.org/alfred_nobel/will/testamente.html (Swedish) and https://www.nobelprize.org/alfred_nobel/will/will-full.html (English)
- Publisher: Nobel Prize Outreach / Nobel Foundation (official)
- Summary: The will asks for the most outstanding work "in an ideal direction." It sets no further criteria.
- Note: The Swedish Academy's English page translates it as "within the field of literature, has produced the most outstanding work in an ideal direction" (https://www.svenskaakademien.se/en/the-nobel-prize-in-literature, publisher: Swedish Academy). Its Swedish page uses the modern spelling: <citation refs="-efICKnxthQjeWYARfZuB">"det utmärktaste i idealisk riktning"</citation> (https://www.svenskaakademien.se/nobelpriset).

**1b. How the Swedish Academy and Nobel Committee judge**
- Process statement: <citation refs="A-7lWPjkp7xJPPk1Viw9K">"In addition to extensive reading and evaluation, members must be open to discussing and considering each other's views."</citation> The same page says each committee member must prepare reports <citation refs="A-7lWPjkp7xJPPk1Viw9K">"evaluating each candidate and arguing for their preferred laureate."</citation>
  - Source: https://www.svenskaakademien.se/en/the-nobel-prize-in-literature/how-are-the-nobel-prize-laureates-in-literature-decided
  - Publisher: Swedish Academy (official)
  - Summary: Selection runs on close reading, committee reports, and discussion. No written scoring rubric appears on the pages I opened.
- On-record criterion (Committee member Ellen Mattson, 2021 interview): <citation refs="3p5mxrK0nweQ-0-Dn_Nc8">"It's all about quality. Literary quality, of course."</citation> and <citation refs="3p5mxrK0nweQ-0-Dn_Nc8">"What we look for is always just excellent literature. It's literary merit. That's the only thing that counts."</citation> She adds: <citation refs="3p5mxrK0nweQ-0-Dn_Nc8">"For me, it's a voice that I hear in the writing that I find within this particular writer's work and nowhere else."</citation>
  - Source: https://www.nobelprize.org/behind-the-scenes-of-the-nobel-prize-in-literature/
  - Publisher: Nobel Prize Outreach (official)
  - Summary: The committee says it judges literary quality and a distinctive, sustained voice, and says it does not weigh the author's personal life.

**1c. Recent prize citations (official English press releases)**
- 2026, Anne Carson: <citation refs="scrcbKSoIKRw0aTBok8BD">"for her bold and inventive oeuvre that, in playful dialogue with the classical tradition, has created new forms for contemporary literature"</citation>. Summary: rewards formal invention in dialogue with the classical tradition.
- 2025, László Krasznahorkai: <citation refs="J2l5zyt3ctmYjzWEzIr1z">"for his compelling and visionary oeuvre that, in the midst of apocalyptic terror, reaffirms the power of art"</citation>. Summary: rewards a visionary body of work confronting apocalyptic themes.
- 2024, Han Kang: <citation refs="lDjNQmXYGfMMo3tyoCMsU">"for her intense poetic prose that confronts historical traumas and exposes the fragility of human life"</citation>. Summary: praises intense, poetic prose that confronts historical trauma.
- 2023, Jon Fosse: <citation refs="aIYh_7z444eR-y2GfbAlP">"for his innovative plays and prose which give voice to the unsayable"</citation>. Summary: praises innovation and giving voice to the unsayable.
- 2022, Annie Ernaux: <citation refs="ZHkXKGcOkeg82uY0mG-ao">"for the courage and clinical acuity with which she uncovers the roots, estrangements and collective restraints of personal memory"</citation>. Summary: praises courage and precision in uncovering personal memory.
- Publisher for all five: Nobel Prize Outreach / Swedish Academy press releases (official). Example URL: https://www.nobelprize.org/prizes/literature/2026/press-release/

## 2. Booker Prize and International Booker Prize

**Booker Prize**
- Official description: <citation refs="riANeQfctMDtuhqLkDrLK">"the best sustained work of fiction written in English and published in the UK and Ireland. The winning book is a work that not only speaks to our current times, but also one that will endure and join the pantheon of great literature."</citation>
  - Source: https://thebookerprizes.com/booker-prize
  - Publisher: Booker Prize Foundation (official)
  - Summary: Awards the best sustained work of fiction, judged by the panel, that the foundation expects to last.
- Entry rules: <citation refs="nzwTtoKpUvnbC7MOeeBR8">"awarded to what is, in the opinion of the judges, the best sustained work of fiction written in English by an author of any nationality, and published in the UK and/or Ireland."</citation>
  - Source: https://thebookerprizes.com/booker-prize/entering-the-booker-prizes
  - Publisher: Booker Prize Foundation (official)
  - Summary: The judges decide "the best" in their own opinion. The rules add no further criteria on this page.
- Judges' words on the 2025 winner, *Flesh* by David Szalay: <citation refs="endRg2yFwIA92Nw9eX5YO">"But somehow, using only the sparest of prose, this hypnotically tense and compelling book becomes an astonishingly moving portrait of a man's life."</citation>
  - Source: https://thebookerprizes.com/the-booker-library/books/flesh
  - Publisher: Booker Prize Foundation (official)
  - Summary: Judges prized spare prose that produces emotional impact.

**International Booker Prize**
- Entry rules: <citation refs="kljcQDm1S8L8E4esKkgYj">"awarded annually to the author and translator of the best … eligible work of long-form fiction or collection of short stories written by an author of any nationality and translated into English, and published in the UK and/or Ireland"</citation>. The page says the best is "in the opinion of the judges."
  - Source: https://thebookerprizes.com/international-booker-prize/enter-the-international-booker-prize
  - Publisher: Booker Prize Foundation (official)
  - Summary: Judges decide the best translated work, and author and translator share the prize.
- Judges' words on the 2026 winner, *Taiwan Travelogue* by Yáng Shuāng-zǐ (trans. Lin King): <citation refs="n4UV74TE-TbJMNFVKx89f">"With sumptuous food writing, laugh-out-loud dialogue and metafictional twists, this novel was impossible to put down."</citation> They add it <citation refs="n4UV74TE-TbJMNFVKx89f">"succeeds as both a delicious romance and an incisive postcolonial novel."</citation>
  - Source: https://thebookerprizes.com/the-booker-library/books/taiwan-travelogue
  - Publisher: Booker Prize Foundation (official)
  - Summary: Judges valued a strong dual achievement (romance and postcolonial critique), vivid writing, and wit.
- Gap: The full Booker rules PDFs did not load, so I could not check them beyond the entry-page summaries.

## 3. Pulitzer Prize for Fiction

- Plan of Award (2026 edition, Prize Board): <citation refs="XPBpDg2G7qTbDKWxOZ5Md">"For distinguished fiction by an American author, preferably dealing with American life, Fifteen thousand dollars ($15,000)."</citation>
  - Source: https://www.pulitzer.org/page/2026-plan-award (category page with the same wording: https://www.pulitzer.org/prize-winners-by-category/219)
  - Publisher: The Pulitzer Prizes / Pulitzer Prize Board, Columbia University (official)
  - Summary: The Plan sets only nationality (American author) and a preference for American subject matter. Eligibility is limited to books first published in the US during the year.
- Caveat: The Plan's "high quality of writing" and "originality" language is attached to the journalism categories, not to Books. Do not read it as a fiction criterion.

## 4. National Book Award and Women's Prize

**National Book Award (fiction)**
- Official text: <citation refs="inuQ9_mr3EC67A1KHx56c">"Panels develop their own criteria for the National Book Award, and discussions are held independent of the Foundation."</citation> It adds that judges <citation refs="inuQ9_mr3EC67A1KHx56c">"may arrive at these choices using whatever criteria they deem appropriate, as long as they do not conflict with the official Award guidelines."</citation>
  - Source: https://www.nationalbook.org/national-book-awards/how-works/
  - Publisher: National Book Foundation (official)
  - Summary: Each five-judge panel sets its own standard. The Foundation publishes no quality criteria.
  - Caveat: The page is undated in the search metadata, so treat the wording as the version I opened.

**Women's Prize for Fiction**
- Terms (2025 edition), section 1.4: <citation refs="aR1daJ9HOLRyRQcy_yTSh">"The judges consider the titles submitted using the guiding tenets of excellence, originality and accessibility. For the purposes of judging, 'excellence' means excellence in quality of writing; 'originality' in perspective and craft; and 'accessibility' means titles that look outwards to a cross-section of readers."</citation>
  - Source: https://womensprize.com/app/uploads/2024/07/Terms_and_Conditions_WP_Fiction_2025.pdf
  - Publisher: Women's Prize Trust (official)
  - Summary: Three written tenets: quality of writing, originality of perspective and craft, and accessibility to a wide readership.
- Judging page: <citation refs="LVKTALMCRKBAjyojto9jm">"basing their deliberations for the longlist, shortlist and winner on three core tenets, which have remained the same since the Prize was founded: excellence, originality and accessibility."</citation>
  - Source: https://womensprize.com/prizes/womens-prize-for-fiction
  - Publisher: Women's Prize Trust (official)
  - Summary: The same three tenets are reaffirmed on the public judging page.
- Caveat: I used the 2025 terms. The 2027 entry conditions PDF was not opened.

## 5. Genre awards

**Hugo Award (Best Novel)**
- Official category text: <citation refs="r6Dz9WbTyWub_ulywoUw9">"Best Novel: Awarded for a science fiction or fantasy story of forty thousand (40,000) words or more."</citation> The page adds that works of <citation refs="r6Dz9WbTyWub_ulywoUw9">"fantasy or horror are eligible if the members of the Worldcon think they are eligible."</citation>
  - Source: https://www.thehugoawards.org/hugo-categories/
  - Publisher: Hugo Awards site (run by Worldcon groups; the rules come from WSFS Constitution Article 3)
  - Summary: Length and genre define eligibility only. No quality criteria are published.
  - Gap: I did not open the WSFS Constitution PDF itself.

**Nebula Award (Novel)**
- Category: <citation refs="uKUjR4nB6IvUUrezBD4BY">"Novel: 40,000 words or more"</citation>. On purpose: <citation refs="uKUjR4nB6IvUUrezBD4BY">"The purpose of the Nebula Awards® is to honor works in each category as chosen by the Full, Associate, and Senior Members (including lifetime and family members) of SFWA as a collective whole."</citation>
  - Source: https://nebulas.sfwa.org/about-the-nebulas/nebula-rules/
  - Publisher: Science Fiction and Fantasy Writers Association (SFWA)
  - Summary: Length defines the novel category. Members vote, and no quality criteria are set. The 2026 rules also bar AI-written work: <citation refs="uKUjR4nB6IvUUrezBD4BY">"All works must be human-authored. Works written or translated, in whole or in part, utilizing generative AI or large language models (LLMs) are not eligible for any Nebula Award®."</citation>

**Edgar Award (Best Novel)**
- Eligibility: <citation refs="94Eg9bdo29Gjg_65RE13E">"Best Novel Hardbound only. First novels by U.S. citizens are not eligible for this category and must be submitted under Best First Novel."</citation>
  - Source: https://mysterywriters.org/edgars/edgar-award-category-information (page headed "Information About 2027 Edgar Awards For Works Published in 2026")
  - Publisher: Mystery Writers of America (official)
  - Summary: Format and eligibility rules only. No quality criteria appear on this page.
- Related genre sub-award: the Lilian Jackson Braun Award lists tone criteria: <citation refs="94Eg9bdo29Gjg_65RE13E">"The story is light in tone, often humorous."</citation> and <citation refs="94Eg9bdo29Gjg_65RE13E">"The crime must be solved in a satisfactory manner by the end of the story."</citation> This is a separate award, not the Best Novel rule.
- Gap: The Edgar judging overview PDF is linked from this page but I did not open it.
